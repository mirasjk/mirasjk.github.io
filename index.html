🎬 nuai-video-editor - Edit Videos Effortlessly on Your Mac

Open-source AI video editor that removes pauses, retakes, filler words and frozen footage. Adds translated subtitles and dubs videos in the speaker's own voice.

✨ Key Features

nuai-video-editor is designed for everyday users who want professional-looking videos without learning complex software. Here's what it does automatically:

  • Removes pauses and retakes: Cuts out awkward silences and repeated sentences.
  • Eliminates filler words: Deletes "um", "uh", and other verbal clutter.
  • Detects and removes frozen footage: Cleans up screen recordings or video calls where nothing moves.
  • Adds translated subtitles: Generates subtitles in multiple languages.
  • Dubs videos in your own voice: Uses voice cloning to replace the original speech with a translation while keeping your voice.

🖥️ System Requirements

To run nuai-video-editor smoothly, your Mac should meet these recommended requirements:

  • Operating System: macOS 12 (Monterey) or later.
  • Processor: Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) or Intel Core i5 or better.
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM minimum, 16 GB recommended.
  • Storage: 2 GB free space for the application and temporary files.
  • Internet: Required for downloading the application and for some AI features that use online models.

🚀 How to Use

Using nuai-video-editor is simple, even if you've never edited a video before:

  1. Open the application from your Applications folder.
  2. Import your video by dragging and dropping it into the app window or using the "Open File" button.
  3. Choose your edits: Select options like "Remove pauses", "Remove filler words", "Add subtitles", or "Dub video".
  4. Click "Process" and wait while the AI works. The time depends on your video length and your Mac's speed.
  5. Preview and export the edited video to your desired folder.

The application guides you through each step with clear buttons and messages. No programming or video editing knowledge is needed.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

If you run into issues, try these common fixes:

  • Application won't open: Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and allow the app to run. You may need to click "Open Anyway".
  • Download fails: Make sure you have a stable internet connection and enough free disk space.
  • Processing is slow: Close other heavy applications. Longer videos take more time.
  • Subtitles are inaccurate: Check that the audio is clear. Background noise can affect recognition.
